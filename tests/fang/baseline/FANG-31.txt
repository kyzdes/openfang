5. WHAT IS AND IS NOT PROVEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROVEN  the daemon runs exactly one Telegram poller; the "two pollers in one
        daemon" hypothesis is refuted for the 45-hour prod cycle (one process,
        one socket, three sequential requests per 64 s cycle).
PROVEN  the 64.02 s period is arithmetic of our own loop:
        2*(LONG_POLL_TIMEOUT + poll_interval) + 1 s conflict latency + INITIAL_BACKOFF.
PROVEN  ChannelAdapter::stop() is never called; poller cancellation is accidental
        (Arc drop), and every hot-reload abandons an in-flight long poll, which
        costs 5 x 409 and 31 s of deafness (reproduced twice on staging).
NOT PROVEN  who the competing reader on the Telegram side is. It cannot be
        identified from the client because the 409 body is discarded, and the
        prod bot may not be probed. The conflict source recurs on a ~60-62 s
        horizon; our 31 s stride quantises it to exactly 64 s.
NOT PROVEN  that fixing the cancellation gap will silence the prod 64 s cycle.
        The fix removes the reload-induced conflicts (measurable, above) and
        makes the remaining ones diagnosable; it is not established that it ends
        the current 45-hour beacon.
